# Ava Wiki

Welcome to the Ava Pro documentation center.

**Ava Pro** is an Android voice assistant app based on the ESPHome protocol, turning your Android device into a smart voice satellite for Home Assistant. Compatible with **Android 5-16**.

Whether setting up a first voice satellite or fine-tuning a multi-device smart home, everything needed is right here.
